Clio

Clio (Κλειώ) is a personal desktop companion for macOS. She sits on the developer's own screen, summarises what is important in his own Gmail inboxes and Google Calendars, and can prepare email drafts and calendar events. Every action that changes anything requires an explicit click on “Yes”.

Clio is not distributed and is not offered to other users. It runs only on the developer's own computer and signs in only to the developer's own Google accounts.
